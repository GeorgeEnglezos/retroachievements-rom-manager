Console logo image sources
==========================

Each <consoleId>.png is a full-color logo downloaded from Wikimedia Commons
(freely reusable media). Files were fetched via the Commons "Special:FilePath"
endpoint and rasterized from the source SVG at 512px wide.

URL pattern used:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/<FILENAME>?width=512

Notes
-----
- Cards use a single fixed light background in both themes. Logos are shown in
  their own colors on it, except three that are light-colored and are tinted to
  ink so they stay legible: 13 (Atari Lynx, yellow), 19 (Wii, grey) and
  27 (Arcade).
- 27 (Arcade) keeps the earlier white monochrome joystick icon (no brand logo
  exists). See CREDITS.md.
- These are brand logos, trademarks of their respective owners, used here for
  identification of each system only.
